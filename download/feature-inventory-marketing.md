## Marketing Pillars (12)

### 1. Member Management & Profiles

**Tagline:** Every Member's Journey — One Screen

**Marketing Points:**
- **Complete Member Profiles** — Full CRUD for every member: contact info, birthday tracking, active/inactive status, and unique member codes (MEM######). Create, view, and edit profiles from one place.
- **Smart Search & Filtering** — Find any member instantly. Active and inactive tabs keep your list clean so you focus on who matters.
- **Birthday Tracking** — See upcoming birthdays at a glance. Send birthday greetings directly via WhatsApp so your members feel valued.
- **Member Health & Goals** — Store height, weight, body fat percentage, emergency contacts, fitness goals, and diet preferences for every member in the CRM.

**Inventory Source:** Member Management, CRM (Member Notes, Interactions, Health)

### 2. Attendance & Check-In

**Tagline:** Check Members In In Seconds — Track Everything Automatically

**Marketing Points:**
- **Instant Check-In** — Search by member name or code and check them in with one click. No paper logs, no clipboards.
- **Self-Service Kiosk** — Turn any tablet into a check-in station. Members search by code or phone and check themselves in — no staff needed. Today's check-in list auto-refreshes every 30 seconds.
- **Full Attendance History** — View today's check-ins or browse historical attendance by any date. Check members out individually to track visit duration.

**Inventory Source:** Attendance Tracking, Self-Service Check-In Kiosk

### 3. Sales & Billing

**Tagline:** Get Paid Faster — Never Lose Track Of A Dollar

**Marketing Points:**
- **One-Click Sale Creation** — Select a member, pick a service (price auto-fills), set the start date, add discount and paid amount — the due amount calculates instantly.
- **Membership Status Control** — Active, frozen, or suspended — see every sale's status with color-coded badges. Freeze or suspend memberships with a reason, and reactivate when ready.
- **Expiry Follow-Ups** — Tabs show you exactly whose membership expires today, tomorrow, in 5 days, in 7 days, or has already expired. Send renewal reminders via WhatsApp in one click.
- **Pending Payment Tracking** — See all outstanding payments in one list. Send WhatsApp payment reminders and record partial or full payments (cash, card, UPI, bank transfer).
- **WhatsApp Invoice Sharing** — Share invoices directly to members' WhatsApp — fast, personal, and proven to get paid faster.
- **PDF Invoices** — Generate branded A4 PDF invoices with line items, payment history, and dynamic currency. Download or print.
- **Invoice Management** — Dedicated invoice page with KPI cards (total invoices, total revenue, outstanding amount), pending tab, and partial payment recording across multiple payment methods.

**Inventory Source:** Sales Management, New Sale, Sales Follow-ups, Pending Payments, Invoice Management, PDF Invoice Generation, WhatsApp Integration

### 11. Security, Roles & Access Control

**Tagline:** Your Data Is Safe — And Only The Right People See It

**Marketing Points:**
- **5 Roles with 40+ Permissions** — SUPER_ADMIN, ADMIN, MANAGER, TRAINER, and MEMBER. Each role has precise access control. Trainers can't see billing; members can't see other members' data.
- **Complete Audit Trail** — Every write operation (create, update, delete, login) is logged with staff ID, organization, action, entity, and details. View org-scoped audit logs filtered by entity type.
- **Organization Data Isolation** — Every API route scopes data by organization. 37 database models are org-scoped. No gym can ever see another gym's data.
- **Secure Authentication** — Staff log in via email and password with cookie-based sessions. Members log in via phone, email, or member code. Role-based redirects ensure everyone lands on the right page.
- **Auth Guards** — Middleware enforces authentication across all routes. Public paths are whitelisted; everything else requires a valid session.

**Inventory Source:** RBAC Permission System, Staff Authentication, Member Authentication, Middleware Auth Guard, Organization Data Isolation, Audit Logging, Audit Log Viewer (Org-level)

### 12. Integrations & Automation

**Tagline:** Connect Everything — Automate Anything

**Marketing Points:**
- **WhatsApp Integration** — Birthday greetings, membership renewal reminders, payment reminders, and invoice sharing — all through WhatsApp using pre-filled wa.me links. Meet your members where they already are.
- **API Keys** — Generate API keys with a `gym_` prefix. View and revoke keys. Build custom integrations with your own tools.
- **Webhooks** — Create webhooks with a URL and event selection. Each webhook gets a `whsec_` secret for verification. Track success and fail counts.
- **Notification System** — View all notification logs: date, recipient, subject, type, and delivery status. Campaigns and system events trigger notifications automatically.
- **Marketing Campaigns** — Create campaigns targeting your members with personalized templates. Execute and track sent/opened counts.

**Inventory Source:** WhatsApp Integration, Integrations (API Keys & Webhooks), Notification System, Marketing Campaigns

## Platform Infrastructure (Behind the Scenes)

These are not customer-facing selling points but demonstrate platform maturity:

- **Self-Service Registration** — Gym owners sign up with gym name, owner details, and plan selection. System creates Organization + Admin Staff + 14-day trial subscription automatically.
- **Subscription Lifecycle** — Platform admins can change plans, extend trials (7/14/30/60/90 days), pause, resume, and cancel subscriptions per tenant. Usage meters track members, staff, and storage.
- **Plan Limits Enforcement** — Member and staff creation is checked against plan limits before allowing the operation.
- **Subscription Write Access Gate** — Write operations are blocked when a subscription is CANCELLED, EXPIRED, or PAUSED.
- **Plan Management** — Create subscription plans with types (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, Unlimited, Custom) and feature lists. Full CRUD with card grid UI.
- **Dark/Light Theme** — Toggle between dark and light modes, persisted per user.
- **PWA Manifest** — Installable on mobile devices as a progressive web app.
- **Application Logging** — Dual-output logger (console + file) with log rotation, level filtering, and a terminal-style viewer.
- **Platform Admin Dashboard** — Overview of all organizations with stats, recent audit logs, and per-org management.
- **Platform Billing Center** — Platform-wide billing with status filters, mark paid/refund actions, and org status panels.

**Inventory Source:** Organization Registration, Subscription Lifecycle Management, Plan Limits Enforcement, Subscription Write Access Gate, Plan Management, Dark/Light Theme, PWA Manifest, Application Logging, Admin Dashboard, Billing Center

## What Makes This Different

This is not a generic business tool repackaged for gyms. Every feature was built specifically for gym operations:

1. **Member codes (MEM######)** and birthday tracking are gym-specific, not generic CRM
2. **Kiosk check-in** with auto-refresh is designed for gym front desks
3. **WhatsApp integration** for renewals and invoices matches how gym owners actually communicate in the Middle East
4. **Rank progression system** (Bronze/Silver/Gold-style) is a gym retention tool, not a business feature
5. **800+ exercise library** with muscle groups and instructions is fitness-domain specific
6. **BMI/BMR/WHR calculators** serve members, not business owners
7. **Class booking with recurring sessions** (daily/weekly/biweekly/monthly) mirrors real gym schedules
8. **Arabic RTL support** with 726+ translated lines addresses the Middle Eastern market directly
9. **Multi-currency per organization** (EGP/USD) serves cross-border operations
10. **Trainer-specific panel** with member assignments reflects gym staff hierarchy
